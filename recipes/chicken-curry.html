Chicken Curry

Description

Chicken curry is a flavorful dish made with chicken, coconut milk, curry powder, vegetables, and different spices.

Ingredients

  • Chicken
  • Coconut milk
  • Curry powder
  • Potatoes
  • Carrots
  • Onion
  • Garlic
  • Cooking oil
  • Salt and pepper

Steps

  1. Heat oil in a pan.
  2. Cook the garlic and onion until soft.
  3. Add the chicken and cook until lightly browned.
  4. Add the curry powder and mix well.
  5. Add the potatoes, carrots, and coconut milk.
  6. Simmer until the chicken and vegetables are fully cooked.
  7. Add salt and pepper according to taste.
  8. Serve hot with rice.
