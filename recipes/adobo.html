Chicken Adobo

Description

Chicken adobo is a popular Filipino dish made with chicken, soy sauce, vinegar, garlic, and other seasonings.

Ingredients

  • Chicken
  • Soy sauce
  • Vinegar
  • Garlic
  • Black pepper
  • Water

Steps

  1. Put the chicken in a pan.
  2. Add soy sauce, vinegar, garlic, and pepper.
  3. Add water and bring it to a boil.
  4. Simmer until the chicken is cooked.
  5. Serve with rice.
